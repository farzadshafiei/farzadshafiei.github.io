About

I trained as a structural engineer and became a mathematics instructor. I hold M.Sc. degrees in Civil (Structural) Engineering from the University of Virginia and in Civil (Earthquake) Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology. Both theses lean on applied mathematics: one on seismic control devices, the other on graph-theoretical optimization of structural matrices.

From Fall 2019 through Spring 2026 I taught undergraduate mathematics as a Lecturer in the Applied Mathematics Center at the University of Virginia. That appointment ended with the Spring 2026 semester. I am seeking teaching and research positions in mathematics and engineering.

Research

Mathematics education for engineering students

With colleagues at UVA I published two studies at the ASEE Annual Conference. The first examined how remote instruction during COVID-19 affected students in applied mathematics courses for engineers. The second tested a confidence-based grading option and measured how it related to students' mathematics confidence.

Shape memory alloy dampers

My M.Sc. thesis at UVA, advised by Prof. O. E. Ozbulut, developed self-centering control devices that use shape memory alloys to reduce seismic response at several hazard levels. A related paper in Smart Materials and Structures (2022) studies steel frames upgraded with NiTiHfPd re-centering dampers.

Optimization with graph theory

My first M.Sc. thesis, advised by Prof. Ali Kaveh, proposed seven graph-theoretical algorithms for improving the conditioning of structural flexibility matrices. This work appeared in Engineering Computations (2022).
